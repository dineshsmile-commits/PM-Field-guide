The big sip

RBI tightens cyber oversight of non-bank payment system operators

Business Standard · 5 November 2026

What happened: On 5 November, RBI issued consolidated Master Directions on Cyber Resilience and Digital Payment Security Controls for non-bank payment system operators, including NPCI, card networks, payment aggregators and wallet issuers. Large and medium operators must comply now; small ones have until 1 April 2028. Operators must get source code for critical third-party apps or use escrow, report incidents within six hours, and apply a 12-hour cooling-off after a customer changes a registered mobile number or email.

Why it matters: Rules like these land directly in the product: onboarding, account recovery, vendor choice and release cadence. One rulebook replacing six older circulars also makes compliance easier to reason about.

If you were the PM: Map every flow that touches a number or email change, and decide how to explain the 12-hour wait so it feels like protection. List your critical vendor apps and check the source-code position for each, since that shapes your release process. Then ask what six-hour incident reporting looks like at 2 am.

Quick sips from six industries

Retail & E-commerce

Beauty Brands Go Quick Commerce-First

Inc42 · 10 November 2026

Redseer puts beauty sales on India's quick commerce apps at about $1 billion in annualised GMV, growing about 90% a year.

For PMs: a report cited by Inc42 says generic makeup searches rose 236% on Blinkit and fell 76% on Amazon, so find where your customers now search.

Pharma & Life Sciences

FDA approves novel Bayer drug to reduce recurrence of stroke

STAT · 9 November 2026

The FDA approved Bayer's Sunharbo, the first of a new drug class, after a trial where 6.2% of patients had a second stroke versus 8.4% on placebo.

For PMs: 53 patients need a year of treatment to prevent one stroke; "number needed to treat" is how doctors and payers judge value.

Health Sciences & MedTech

The Big Challenge for AI in Healthcare Comes After Approval

MedCity News (contributor piece) · 6 November 2026

The author notes roughly 1,450 FDA-authorised AI devices and cites an eye-screening system that gave no result for a quarter of patients because of camera quality and pupil size.

For PMs: plan post-launch monitoring and change control before launch day.

Telecom & Connectivity

Can Jio's stock market debut reset India's telecom pricing?

Light Reading · 30 October 2026

Jio Platforms' $3.2 billion IPO is called India's largest, and media reports say Nomura expects a possible 15% tariff hike in November–December 2026.

For PMs: after its July 2024 price rise Jio lost around 12.74 million subscribers in three months; every tariff call trades ARPU against churn.

Fintech & Banking

ChatGPT's new Wallet lets AI agents shop and check out for you

TechCrunch · 13 November 2026

OpenAI launched a wallet that lets ChatGPT's shopping agents complete purchases, starting with some US ChatGPT Pro subscribers.

For PMs: note the trust design: a review screen, Face ID-style approval, and payment tokens that keep card details from the agent and merchant.

B2B SaaS & Enterprise

Adobe brings AI agents and new creative tools to Photoshop, Premiere, and Firefly

TechCrunch · 10 November 2026

At its MAX event, Adobe announced an MCP server that lets chatbots such as ChatGPT, Claude or Gemini use Adobe features.

For PMs: letting outside assistants call your features buys distribution but loosens control of the experience.

Framework of the fortnight

Kano model, applied to ChatGPT's Wallet

Kano sorts features into basics users expect, performance features where more is better, and delighters nobody asked for. In ChatGPT's Wallet, an agent that buys for you is the delighter. The review screen, biometric approval and payment token are basics: nobody praises them, but one failure breaks trust. OpenAI plans spending limits and category controls later. Kano suggests those will soon be basics too, so they deserve priority over more delight. Explore Kano and the other frameworks.

Something to chew on

"You are the PM for a payment app. RBI's rules require a 12-hour cooling-off period after a user changes their registered mobile number or email. How would you implement it without losing users?"

A strong answer covers:

  • The user and the risk: who changes numbers, and how the wait blocks account takeover.
  • The experience: warn before the change, show a countdown, and say what still works during the wait.
  • The metrics: drop-off and support tickets against fraud losses, with edge cases checked with compliance.

Last sip

Payment rules, shopping agents and medical AI all said the same thing this fortnight: people trust products that plan for what can go wrong.
