Your route in

A four-stage path into a domain PM role

Stage 1

Learn the domain

Read the industry brief. Learn the regulators, the money flow and who the real customer is. In pharma, the prescriber and the payer are often not the patient.

Stage 2

Practise the toolkit

Learn the first principles, then run each framework on a real product from your chosen industry. Write one page per framework, using the worked examples as a model.

Stage 3

Build a case study

Pick one problem, for example drop-off at UPI checkout. Take it from discovery through to a metric you'd track. This becomes your portfolio piece.

Stage 4

Speak the language

Use the trend questions for interview practice. Being able to discuss DSCSA, FHIR or network slicing sets domain candidates apart.
